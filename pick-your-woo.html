How your picks stay fresh

All three covers draw from the same collection. A message is chosen randomly when you click, not assigned by the date or the card. The other two cards do not use up any messages.

This browser remembers only the IDs of Woos you have drawn. Each is set aside until you have explored the whole library. Then a new round begins, avoiding the most recent picks where possible. A finite library eventually repeats; it is not an endless supply.

History stays in this browser and is not sent to Annie or a website account. Clearing site data, resetting history, using another browser or device, or ending a private-browsing session can remove repeat protection. When storage is blocked, protection lasts only in the current page. Picks made before this upgrade were not recorded.

These are original reflections, not predictions, readings or personal instructions. No signup is needed. The rest of the website, including your field notebook, is unaffected by this history.
