# WooWooish brand guide

## The promise

A little space for **calm, peace and love** in everyday life. Spiritual curiosity with both feet on the ground. Nature, meditation, reflection and small acts of kindness are invitations, not requirements.

Frame this as remembering what is already within, rather than achieving a new self or completing a spiritual journey. Annie’s distinctive introduction is “aerospace by day, woo woo-ish by night.” She works in aerospace; do not imply she is an engineer, scientist, therapist or spiritual teacher. Curiosity about consciousness and spirituality should stay curious, not certain.

## Voice

Warm, curious, playful and plain-spoken. Sound like an invitation from a thoughtful friend. Short sentences, specific moments and a little lightness. Welcome the whole range of feelings; no pressure to be positive or spiritually accomplished.

Good phrases: “come as you are,” “a little space,” “keep what resonates,” “let small be enough.” Avoid guarantees of healing, language that blames people for difficult feelings, exaggerated spiritual authority, or claims that the ocean or a practice treats a condition.

Content should offer something useful without demanding a signup: a short reflection, a question to carry into a walk, or a written practice that works without JavaScript. Do not turn a personal exploration into invented professional credentials.

## Design

Preserve the approved reference: deep navy, aqua and ocean blue; Bricolage Grotesque with Space Mono accents; oversized compact headlines; circular portraits; curved shapes, scallops and pill buttons. Use the original Annie portraits. New decorative artwork should belong to this visual language and be identified as decoration, not an actual post thumbnail or event photograph.

Use readable text, visible keyboard focus, comfortable tap targets, responsive layouts and reduced-motion support. The pause should never autoplay, force a breathing pattern or require sound. Reflections should use native accessible disclosures.

Sharing previews must use the portrait-free WooWooish card (`assets/woowooish-share-20261006.png`) and brand icons. Do not use Annie’s face or personal portraits in Open Graph, Twitter card or mobile preview metadata. Keep the approved preview title “WooWooish” and description “A little reminder of what’s already within. 🤍”.

## Facts and privacy

Only publish confirmed dates, places, prices, reel links, testimonials, qualifications and other factual claims. Gathering ideas are explicitly interest options until a schedule is supplied. The newsletter is an interest request until an actual subscription service is connected. Do not claim a message was sent or a place booked when the site only prepared an email draft.

Do not publish private personal history, sensitive health information, financial circumstances, private locations or messages from prior conversations. Do not add tracking or collect visitor data through an unapproved service.

## Useful content themes

- Calm in the middle of a busy day: small pauses, natural breathing, ordinary surroundings.
- Peace with what is here: naming a feeling gently, making room without forcing a silver lining.
- Love in ordinary actions: kindness toward ourselves, friends and the wider world.
- Everyday wonder: noticing nature, light, sound and the small details of being alive.

The site’s original reflections are brand writing commissioned by the user, not quotations or verified Instagram posts. Avoid labeling them as already published elsewhere.
