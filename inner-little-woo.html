Inner Little Woo

A safe space for all your feelings. ♡

When strong emotions arise, you can come here to pause, listen, and care for the part of you that needs a little love.

Meet your Inner Little Woo — a gentle way to welcome the scared, sad, angry, or ashamed parts of yourself without rushing to change them.

A gentle practice

How it works

1

Notice

Pause and notice what you're feeling in your body, mind, and heart.

2

Invite

Say hello to your Little Woo. Let them know it's okay to come out when ready.

3

Listen

Let the feeling speak, whether it's sadness, anger, fear, shame, or something else.

4

Care

Ask: How are you feeling? Do you need a hug? How can I help?

5

Integrate

Check in again. Reflect, ground yourself, or simply continue your day.

6

Come back anytime

There's no perfect ending. Every feeling is welcome, as often as you need.

You don't have to make your feelings go away.
You can learn to meet them with love. 🤍

This is a reflective wellness practice, not therapy or crisis support. If exploring feelings becomes overwhelming, stop and ground yourself or seek support from someone you trust.
