02 / the three-minute pause

Nothing to achieve. Nothing to sign up for. Just three small moments to meet yourself where you are.

Sit or stand somewhere comfortable. Keep your eyes open if you like, and let your breathing stay natural. The words below are your guide; move at your own pace.

a moment, just for you 3:00

Begin whenever you’re ready.

  1. 01

    Arrive.

    Feel your feet and the support beneath you. Let your shoulders soften if that feels good. You can be here exactly as you are.

    the first minute
  2. 02

    Notice.

    What can you hear? What do you feel? Thoughts can come and go. There’s no need to fix anything; simply notice one small thing.

    the second minute
  3. 03

    Be kind.

    Ask yourself: what is one kind thing I can offer myself next? A glass of water, a little fresh air, a gentler word. Let small be enough.

    the third minute

calm01 / a little calm

Calm in the middle of life

You don’t have to wait for everything to settle before taking a small pause.

Read reflection

The inbox may still be full. The laundry may still be waiting. A quiet moment doesn’t need a perfect setting. Put both feet on the floor, notice the light in the room and let this one moment be simple. You can return to the rest when you’re ready.

Where could you make a little room today?

peace02 / a little peace

Meet the feeling gently

There is room for your whole day here, even the messy parts.

Read reflection

Some days feel bright. Some arrive heavy or a little tangled. You don’t have to turn every feeling into a lesson or hurry toward a silver lining. Try naming what is here without judging it. “This is a hard moment” can be a kinder beginning than “I should feel better.”

What would a gentler sentence sound like?

Make a little space →
love03 / a little love

Love already lives here

Start with one ordinary act of kindness. Let it count.

Read reflection

Love can look like checking on a friend, making yourself something nourishing or listening without planning what to say next. It doesn’t always need a grand gesture. Notice the care already woven into your day, and choose one small way to offer a little more.

Who could use a little kindness, including you?

Make a little space →
wonder04 / a little wonder

Being fully alive

A little attention can turn an ordinary walk into something worth remembering.

Read reflection

Look for a color you hadn’t noticed. Listen for a sound beyond the usual rush. Feel the air on your skin. There is no right way to be present, and no need to make the moment profound. Sometimes being here is enough: curious, imperfect and awake to one small beautiful thing.

What is one thing you noticed today?
