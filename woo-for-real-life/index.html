A card can offer a spark. These guides give you somewhere to stay with it for a few minutes. Choose a recognizable situation, not the feeling you think you ought to have.

Each guide names the moment, offers one useful distinction, and suggests one small, optional practice. There is one question to carry and one relevant existing Woo at the end. No lesson to pass. No long list of things to do next.

Read from the beginning or go straight to the practice. Keep your eyes open, let your breathing be natural, and skip anything that does not suit you. You can leave with a sentence rather than a solution.

Here, Woo means a little room for attention, kindness, and ordinary wonder. You do not need a spiritual belief to use these guides. They do not predict outcomes, explain someone else’s motives, or ask you to treat every disappointment as something that was meant to happen.

This is original brand writing, not a collection of Annie’s personal experiences or professional advice. For unsafe situations, ongoing distress, or problems needing specialist help, a reflection is not a substitute for appropriate support.
